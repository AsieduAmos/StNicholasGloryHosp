The Patient's Rights ✔

➽ The patient has the right to quality basic health care irrespective of the
patient's geographical location.
➽ The patient is entitled to full information on the patient's condition and
management.
➽ The patient is entitled to the alternative treatment and other health care
providers within the service.
➽ The patient has the right to know the identity of the caregivers and other
persons who may handle the patient.
➽ The patient has the right to consent or decide to participate in a proposed
research study involving the patient after a full explanation has been given.
➽ Patient who declines to participate in or withdraws from a research project
is entitled to the most effective care available.
➽ Patient has the right to privacy during the consultation, examination and
treatment.
➽ The patient is entitled to confidentiality of information obtained about the
patient and that information shall not be disclosed to third party without
the consent of the patient.
➽ The patient is entitled to the relevant information regarding policies and
regulation of the health facilities that the patient attends.
➽ Precedures for complaints, disputs and conflict resolution shall be explained
to patients or thier accredited respectives.
➽ Hospital charges, mode of payment and the forms of anticipated expenditure
shall be explained to the patient prior to treatment.
➽ The patient is entitled to personal safty and reasonable security of property
within the confines of the institution.
➽The patient has the right to a second medical opinion if the patient so desires.
